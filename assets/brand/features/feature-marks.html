Three marks from the same water

Splash's mark is a drop over the ripple it leaves. These three are drawn the same way: a 32‑unit grid, flat solid shapes, 3‑unit round strokes, one ink. They sit beside the Splash mark without competing with it, and still read at 16 pixels.

Renaming Trails

Trails becomes Delta

  • Splash, the delta browser for macOS.
  • Every page you open branches from the one before it. Your delta keeps the whole shape.
  • Drag a page onto another to move its branch.

"Trail" is a fine word, but it's the hiking metaphor and Splash is a water brand. A delta is the shape water makes when one channel splits into many, which is exactly the parent and child structure the sidebar shows. It's one short word, sounds like a technology, and keeps "branch" as its natural verb.

The rename touches about 790 mentions in 136 source files and 81 on the site. I'd change what people read (menus, Settings, palette, onboarding, site) and leave code names like SharedTrails alone, so nothing breaks.
