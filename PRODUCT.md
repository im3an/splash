# Splash product

## Register

product

## Users

People browsing on macOS who keep related research, reading and daily work open across multiple pages. They need to follow a topic without losing its ancestry and return to their place after switching workspaces or restarting.

## Product Purpose

A Chromium browser whose persistent parent–child trails replace a flat tab strip. The trail is the source of truth; workspaces hold a trail, its active page and its visual identity. Daily browsing should feel calm, fast and dependable.

## Brand Personality

Considered, quiet, personal. Use plain, specific language and native macOS conventions. Preserve the existing compact shell, warm paper and graphite themes, workspace-tinted frame, Lucide icons and system typography.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboard cards, promotional hero layouts, decorative effects, noisy status badges, unfamiliar controls, and visual reinvention that obscures the existing browsing workflow. A polished happy path with silent failures does not meet the product bar.

## Design Principles

- The trail is the product. Keep relationships and page state visible together.
- Every important action communicates what happened, what is happening and the next useful step.
- Protect progress and offer undo or recovery. Keep entered values after a failed operation.
- Use one coherent vocabulary and interaction system across browsing, panels and settings.
- Preserve context across workspaces, overlays and panel navigation. Reduce surprise and unnecessary motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor system and in-app reduced motion. Provide keyboard operation, visible focus, accessible control names, readable text and non-color status cues. Target WCAG 2.1 AA for shell interfaces; do not claim conformance without measuring and testing the running app.
