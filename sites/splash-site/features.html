Everything in Splash

Every feature in today's beta, grouped the way you'll meet them, with what's coming at the end. Shortcuts are on the right.

Trails

Pages keep the page they came from. The trail replaces the tab strip.

  • Links grow a branch

    Open a link and it becomes a child of the page you were on. Links within the same page stay put.

  • Child pages

    ⌥⌘T

    Start a new page under the current one, ready for an address. ⌃⌘T starts one beside it instead.

  • Fold, rename and note

    F2

    Collapse branches, rename any page and keep a note on it.

  • Pinned pages

    ⌘D

    Pin a page to the top of its trail. Closing it puts it to sleep instead of losing it.

  • Drag to rearrange

    Drag pages within the trail, onto a group, onto another workspace, or into a bookmarks folder. A line shows where the page will land.

  • Groups

    Gather trails under a named group with its own icon. Drop a page on any group's heading, even a folded one.

  • Name a trail

    Give the first page of a trail an emoji, so a long sidebar is easy to scan.

  • Jump between trails

    ⇧⌘↓

    ⇧⌘↓ and ⇧⌘↑ go to the next or previous trail, ⇧⌘← and ⇧⌘→ fold and unfold a branch, and ⌥⌘C folds every other trail.

  • Move from the keyboard

    ⌥⌘↑

    Reorder, nest and un-nest pages with ⌥⌘ and the arrow keys. Arrows alone walk the trail.

  • Undo every change

    ⌘Z

    Closes, moves and renames come back with ⌘Z. ⇧⌘Z redoes them.

  • Filter the trail

    ⌥⌘F

    Shows matching pages with their ancestors. Return opens the first match.

  • Close a branch

    ⌥⌘W

    Close Branch, Close Pages Below or Close Other Pages, each with Undo. ⇧⌘K clears the workspace's loose pages and keeps pinned and grouped ones.

  • Folded rows keep their state

    A collapsed branch shows how many pages it hides and any sound, download or error inside.

  • Export a trail

    Save a trail as a Markdown outline, or paste an outline of links to rebuild one.

  • Where you left off

    Back after an hour away with several trails open, Splash offers a summary of where you stopped in each one.

  • More than one window

    ⌘N

    Every window shows the same trails. Move a page to a new window, or to one that's already open.

Search and get around

One box finds anything, and the keyboard reaches everything.

  • Search everything

    ⌘K

    Open pages in every workspace, bookmarks, history and commands. The selected row says what Return will do, like “Switch to Studio”.

  • New page, one field

    ⌘T

    ⌘T opens the same palette: type an address or a search to open it as a new page, or pick a page you already have.

  • Commands

    >

    Start with > to search commands only. Toggles name what they will do, like “Unpin page”. Tab switches between All, Commands and Shortcuts.

  • Every shortcut

    ⌘/

    The palette lists every keyboard shortcut, grouped. Type ? in the palette to search them.

  • Address suggestions

    ⌘L

    Bookmarks, history and search suggestions as you type. The field can sit in the toolbar or the sidebar. ⌥⌘V pastes an address and goes.

  • Waymarks

    w ␣

    A short word and a space searches one site: w for Wikipedia, gh for GitHub. 313 more to add in Settings, from a list that ships with Splash.

  • Step through pages

    ⌃⇥

    Move through visible rows. ⌘1–8 picks a row and ⌘9 jumps to the last.

  • Back, forward, swipe

    ⌘[

    Mouse buttons and three-finger swipes work too, and history follows the trail.

  • Find and zoom

    ⌘F

    Find in the page. ⌘+, ⌘− and ⌘0 set the zoom.

  • History

    ⌘Y

    Search, reopen or remove sites from the sidebar, with Undo. Recently closed pages are listed too.

  • Bookmarks

    ⌥⌘B

    Search, rename, move and delete many at once, or save a whole branch as a folder. ⇧⌘B shows the bookmarks bar.

Workspaces

Reading, a trip and work each keep their own trail and look.

  • Separate trails

    ⌃1–9

    Each workspace remembers its trail, selected page and split view.

  • Your colours

    Pick up to three colours on the wheel or start from a preset. The frame gets a gradient and a little grain.

  • Light or dark

    Warm charcoal to start with, warm paper, or follow macOS. A workspace can keep its own, and websites follow the theme too.

  • Move pages across

    Drag a page onto a workspace or use Move to Workspace, with Go there and Undo.

  • Rename, reorder, delete

    Manage workspaces from the sidebar or Settings. Deleting one can be undone.

Alma view

Every page in a workspace drawn as a map. In preview, not in the beta yet.

  • Open the map

    ⌥⌘G

    From the View menu, ⌘K or the sidebar button. The sidebar stays live beside it.

  • Seven modes

    1–7

    Web, Sites, Folders, Types, Trails, Rings and Focus: the same pages drawn seven ways.

  • Focus on a page

    F

    Where it came from, where it led and its site's other pages, one to three steps out.

  • Filter with a query

    /

    Words plus is:, site:, folder:, type:, trail:, depth: and mem:. A minus turns any term around.

  • Lenses

    One click for Duplicates, Heaviest, Asleep, Sound, Failed, Lone or Deep pages.

  • Act on many pages

    ⇧↵

    Pick pages or every match, then open, focus, split, move, copy as Markdown or close, with Undo.

  • Drag to re-nest

    Drop a page on another to move it under that page in its trail.

  • Draw it your way

    S

    Icons, Stars or Ink, colour by trail, site, folder or type, size by what a page led to or by memory, and set how hard pages spread, pull and gather.

  • Motion

    Drift, Flow along the trails, and a replay that grows the map from the first page. Still with reduced motion on.

Reading, notes and media

Compare pages, read without clutter, write things down, watch and listen.

  • Split view

    ⌘\

    Two related pages side by side, the parent on the left. The pair survives a restart.

  • Reader

    ⌥⌘R

    Just the article, in serif text and Splash's own light or dark, with the author and reading time. A− and A+ set the size.

  • Notes

    ⌥⌘N

    Notes that know which sites they're about. Type / for headings, to-dos, callouts, tables, code, maths, diagrams and sketches. Kept as Markdown.

  • Notes on the page you're on

    Link a note to a site, or paste a link into it, and the Notes button shows a count whenever you're back on that site.

  • Assistant panel

    ⇧⌘A

    Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Le Chat beside the page, signed in with your own account.

  • Picture in picture

    ⌥⌘P

    Float a video over your other pages while you keep reading. Also from a video's right-click menu.

  • Sound on the trail

    ⌃⌘M

    Rows show which page is playing. Click to mute. Media keys and Now Playing work as on macOS.

  • Full-screen video

    A site's full screen fills the window, even from split view. Esc gives it back.

  • Open files

    ⌘O

    PDFs, images, text, audio and video open as pages, and come back after a restart.

  • Print and save

    ⌘P

    Print, save a single-file web archive with ⇧⌘S, or view source with ⌥⌘U.

  • Share and copy

    ⇧⌘C

    Copy the page address with a confirmation, copy it as a Markdown link with ⌥⇧⌘C, or use the macOS share menu.

  • Spelling

    Follows your macOS language, or pick one in Settings. Add words to the dictionary from the right-click menu.

  • Task manager

    Memory and CPU for every page across your windows, from the View menu. End a page that's misbehaving.

Privacy and security

Protective by default, and quiet about it.

  • Ads and trackers blocked

    uBlock Origin is built in, with its filter lists.

  • Cookie banners rejected

    Consent pop-ups are answered with “reject”, never accepted on your behalf.

  • Block third-party cookies

    One switch in Settings, with exceptions per site. It starts off, because some sign-ins and embedded players need them.

  • Cookies per site

    See which sites keep cookies, and delete one site's or all of them, from Settings.

  • Tracking parameters removed

    Tags like fbclid and gclid come off an address before the request leaves your Mac.

  • Pop-ups kept in check

    Only a click or a key press can open a pop-up. Allow or block them per site, and open a blocked one from the address bar.

  • HTTPS first

    Public sites are tried over HTTPS before anything is sent. HTTP only if you choose to continue.

  • Secure DNS

    Encrypted lookups through Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad or Google Public DNS, or your network's own DNS, encrypted when it can be. A strict mode never falls back.

  • Dangerous-site warnings

    Checked against lists kept on your Mac and refreshed every 12 hours. Your addresses aren't sent anywhere.

  • Private windows

    ⇧⌘N

    Browse without keeping history, cookies or site data afterwards.

  • Per-site permissions

    Camera, microphone, location, JavaScript, images and autoplay, decided and remembered per site.

  • Clear browsing data

    Clear by time range, with Undo for history, or clear automatically when you quit.

  • Ask sites not to track you

    Sends Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. On by default.

  • Certificate warnings

    Continuing past a warning applies to that one certificate only. Sites that require HTTPS are never bypassed.

  • Sandboxed pages

    Websites run sandboxed, with no access to Splash itself or your files.

  • Links to other apps

    mailto:, tel: and similar links ask before opening an app, and remember your answer per site.

Passwords and forms

Sign in and check out without retyping.

  • Password vault

    Saved logins are sealed with a key held in your macOS Keychain. A copy of the database reveals nothing.

  • Autofill

    Fill logins, addresses and cards on the page, from a small menu next to the field. Cards ask for Touch ID first.

  • Strong passwords

    On a sign-up form, Splash suggests a strong password, fills it in and saves it straight away, so it can't get lost.

  • Sign-in prompts

    Sites that ask for a server password get a native sign-in sheet that names the server.

  • Import and export logins

    Bring logins in from a CSV file and export them again whenever you want.

Downloads

Every file, where you can see it.

  • Progress at a glance

    ⌥⌘L

    A downloads popover, and progress on the row of the page the file came from.

  • Sorted by kind

    Documents, images, video, audio, archives and apps, each easy to find.

  • Retry with a reason

    Failed downloads say why and offer Retry. Finished ones show in Finder.

  • Unsafe-file warnings

    Risky file types ask before they're kept.

Extensions and import

Bring your tools and your bookmarks with you.

  • Chrome Web Store

    Install Chrome extensions from the store or a folder, and manage them in Settings. Some Chrome APIs aren't available yet.

  • Import from other browsers

    Bookmarks and history from Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Firefox and Safari.

  • Export bookmarks

    Save bookmarks as a standard HTML file that any browser can read.

  • Default browser

    Make Splash your default, and links from other apps open in your current workspace.

Reliability

Your place survives restarts, crashes and slow pages.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Pages, workspaces and the active page come back after a restart. Or start fresh each time.

  • Saved as you go

    Trail changes are saved continuously. If a save fails, Splash says so and retries.

  • Typing is protected

    Pages with edited fields warn before closing and stay awake instead of sleeping.

  • Sleeping pages

    Pages you aren't using sleep to save memory, more of them when your Mac is short, and wake when opened.

  • Stuck pages recover

    An unresponsive page gets a bar to wait or reload, without taking the window down.

  • Back online by itself

    Pages that failed offline reload once your Mac reconnects. Other errors say what happened and offer Try again.

  • Safe upgrades

    Your data is copied before every database upgrade, and damaged settings are recovered field by field.

  • Diagnostics

    Export a diagnostics file from About when something goes wrong.

On the way

Planned and being worked on, in no fixed order. Plans can change.

  • Alma view in the beta

    ⌥⌘G

    The map of your pages, in preview today, arrives in beta builds once it's ready.

  • Automatic updates

    Once builds are signed by Apple, Splash updates itself and says what changed in About.

  • Passkeys

    Sign in with passkeys and Touch ID. Until then, Splash tells you when a site asks for one.

  • Resizable split view

    Drag the divider between two pages, and later put more than two side by side.

  • Ask about this page

    Summarise the page you're on, or ask about what you've selected, in the assistant panel.

  • Your own shortcuts

    Change the keys for any command.

  • Logins per workspace

    Each workspace signed in to its own accounts, so work and home stay apart.

  • Translation

    Translate a page, offered only when it's in a language you don't read.

  • Sync between Macs

    Your trails on every Mac you use. Later, after profiles.

Not yet

What the beta can't do today, said out loud.

  • Some streaming video

    Netflix, Disney+ and Spotify on the web may not play, because Electron doesn't include Widevine.

  • Intel Macs

    The beta runs on Macs with Apple silicon only.

  • Some extensions

    A few Chrome extension features aren't available yet, so some extensions may not work fully.

  • Sync

    Trails, bookmarks and passwords stay on the Mac they were made on.
